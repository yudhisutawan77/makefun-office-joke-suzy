[One-Line Office Joke Laugh-Off](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suzy-one-line-office-joke-laugh-off-f574ac73)
on make.fun.

## Entry

> I asked the office AI to draft my resignation letter, and it asked for two weeks' notice to think about it.

A single line (107 characters). The setup asks the office AI for help
quitting; the punchline flips the roles — the AI itself demands the
two weeks' notice. Specific to modern office life, fresh angle, and
the twist lands on the last words.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (or to the path named
by `ENTRY_OUT`, or `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`). It validates the entry
first: one line, 1–200 characters, valid UTF-8, not blank.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
